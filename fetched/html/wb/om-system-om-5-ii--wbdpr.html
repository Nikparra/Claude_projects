Product description
Announced Jun 17, 2025
Discuss in the Micro Four Thirds Talk forum

The OM-5 II is an entry-level mirrorless camera from OM System. It features a 20MP Four Thirds CMOS sensor, which is stabilized up to 7.5EV when paired with Sync IS lenses. Compared to its predecessor, it gains a USB-C port for charging and OM System's latest menu design.

Our review

The OM-5 II still offers some of the most features at its price point, but doesn't nail the basics as well as its competitors.

Good for: Travelers who need a small kit Outdoor enthusiasts IBIS fans

Not so good for: Sports and action shooters Videographers
