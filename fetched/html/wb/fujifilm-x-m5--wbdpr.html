Product description
Announced Oct 14, 2024
Discuss in the Fujifilm X System / SLR Talk forum

The Fujifilm X-M5 is an entry-level X-mount camera built around the company's 26MP X-Trans CMOS sensor. It features Fujifilm's latest AI-powered subject detection modes, an articulating screen and a dedicated vlogging mode.
The camera has a microphone, headphone, and USB-C port, as well as MicroHDMI.

Our review

The X-M5 nails most of the basics for taking photos and videos, especially for beginners.

Good for: Beginner photographers and fans of the 'Gram

Not so good for: Shooting fast-moving subjects or in bright light
