Product description
Announced Apr 3, 2023
Discuss in the Third Party Lens Talk forum

The Sigma 23mm F1.4 DC DN Contemporary is a new wide angle prime for APS-C camera systems, where it is equivalent to 35mm. It has a high-speed stepping motor for smooth and quiet focusing during video recording. It has two aspherical lens elements along with three special low dispersion elements, nine rounded aperture blades and a minimum focus distance of 25cm/9.8".
