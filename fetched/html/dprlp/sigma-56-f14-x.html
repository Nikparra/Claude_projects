Product description
Announced Jun 22, 2020
Discuss in the Fujifilm X System / SLR Talk forum

The Sigma 56mm F1.4 DC DN is a fast portrait prime for Sony E, Leica L, Fujifilm X and Nikon-Z APS-C systems. It's constructed of 10 elements in six groups, has a minimum focus distance of 50cm (20") and weighs around 280g (9.9oz).
