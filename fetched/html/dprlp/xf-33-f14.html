Product description
Announced Sep 2, 2021
Discuss in the Fujifilm X System / SLR Talk forum

The Fujifilm XF 33mm F1.4 R LM WR is a fast standard prime lens that's equivalent to 50mm when mounted on an X-series body. It features 15 elements, including both aspherical and 'ED" elements. Its linear motor drives the focus group quickly and quietly. The lens has a minimum focus distance of 30cm (11.8") and a max magnification of 0.15x. It's weather-sealed, features an aperture ring and weighs just 360 grams (12.7 oz).
