FUJIFILM X-Pro3

PURE PHOTOGRAPHY

Something different is here. This camera turns anticipation into reality.
The texture of titanium stimulates your senses, while the unique viewfinder prompts discovery and creativity.
It brings back the desire to interact with the world through a camera,
while attaining an understanding to record it as your own for eternity. The X-Pro3 is the definition of pure photography.

Made from titanium,
ready for any environment

TITANIUM + MAGNESIUM

The frame of the camera body is made from magnesium, while the top cover and the base plate, which are the parts exposed to the elements are made from corrosion resistant titanium.

WEATHER RESISTANT

Street photographers prefer to carry their camera on them rather than putting it in a bag, to make sure they are ready to capture those fleeting moments that will never exist again. What they need is a compact, lightweight and reliable camera, allowing them to focus on capturing the world around them.

Take pictures without distractions,
be in the moment,
through the viewfinder

Take pictures without distractions,
be in the moment, through the viewfinder

Enjoy taking pictures without distractions

The hidden LCD encourages a more traditional shooting style, asking photographers to concentrate on composing using the viewfinder. Of course, you can flip open the screen to check your images between shooting sessions, but in essence, the X-Pro3 wants you to trust your instincts as a photographer and shoot without the distraction of checking every image. The LCD screen helps you to adjust the settings and find what needs to be improved, ready for next time.

The LCD screen has an anti-reflective coating, a wide angle of view, and is able to display images with high contrast and natural colours, ideal for reviewing or composing images in bright daylight or low light.

Digital representation of film-based visual effects is something only allowed for iconic photographic equipment. The visual effects appeal to users’ emotions and inspire imagination.
The use of Memory LCD means users can easily see the settings for Film Simulation, white balance, etc. regardless of whether the camera is turned off or on.

The latest performance
with a traditional style

The new firmware algorithm in the X-Pro3 is designed to harness
the full potential of the fourth-generation X-Trans CMOS 4 sensor and X-Processor 4.

LOW-LIGHT AF

The evolution of digital cameras is slowly eliminating the limits of low light shooting. The X-Pro3's AF system operates down to -6EV, enabling shooting in situations that have been deemed "pitch darkness" until now. The AF system has evolved to be able to auto-focus on not only what the eyes can see but also what your mind senses.

©Tomasz Lazar

HDR MODE

The X-Pro3 can combine images to expand the dynamic range (DR). It is a powerful tool that photographically reproduces the wider dynamic range that occurs naturally, yet is capable of producing a new type of HDR with smooth tonality and beautiful colors.

Tomoharu Shimada

MULTI EXPOSURE

The camera can merge up to 9 frames in Additive, Average, Comparative Bright or Comparative Dark modes, combining images taken from multiple viewpoints or at different times into a single image. You can apply different Film Simulations to each of the frames, and paste colour fragments onto monochrome pictures to create a multi-layered collage.

©Yukio Uchida

Masters of colour
for over 85 years

FUJIFILM has continued to study colour since the production and development of photographic films in 1934. The technologies and experiences, accumulated over the years, have been injected into the Film Simulation modes. Each of the carefully chosen modes has a unique set of colours and tonality to create images that reflect how a photographer felt when taking the picture. The world of extended colour expressions including CLASSIC CHROME, ACROS and ETERNA, is now joined by another new mode of Film Simulation.

The new Film Simulation mode has been designed to simulate colour negative film that was traditionally chosen for snapshots of everyday scenes. The colours, precisely controlled for each level of brightness, create a rich chromatic contrast to add extra definition. The new "CLASSIC Neg." takes you back to scenes as you saw them growing up, bringing back the joy of photography.

©Cyril Abad

©Yukio Uchida

©Eric Bouvet

©Patrick La Roque

©Pan Wang

Monochromatic Colour

The X-Pro3 allows you to choose a key color from a matrix of Warm / Cool tones and Magenta / Green hues. You can incorporate the rich gradation into your images to create photographs with a personal twist. Enjoy creating your own monochrome world with added hues, going beyond the traditional approach of using warm tones for a retro look and cool tones for urban snapshots.

©Yukio Uchida

©Matias Costa

Clarity setting

"Clarity" is the third parameter after Tone and Sharpness. It controls a picture's impression while retaining gradation.

Colour Chrome Effect Blue

This adds more depth and naturally enhances the vividness of the colour.

©Megan Lewis
